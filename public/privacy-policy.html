1. Overview

Pet Genie ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Pet Genie mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By downloading, installing, or using Pet Genie, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

🐾 Our Promise

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide you with the best pet health analysis experience. Your pet's data is never sold to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

Data Type Purpose

Pet photos AI health analysis & scan results
Pet profile (breed, age, weight) Personalized health recommendations

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Usage data (features accessed, scan frequency)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics
  • IP address and general location (country/region level only)

2.3 Camera & Photo Access

Pet Genie requires camera access solely to capture photos of your pet for AI health analysis. Photos are processed to generate health reports and are stored securely in your account. We never access your full photo library — only photos you explicitly capture or select within the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide AI health analysis — Process pet photos through our AI models to generate health reports
  • Personalize recommendations — Tailor diet, exercise, and care guides based on your pet's breed and age
  • Track health history — Store past scan results so you can monitor changes over time
  • Send smart care reminders — Notify you about vaccinations, check-ups, and seasonal care tips
  • Improve our AI — Use anonymized, aggregated data to enhance analysis accuracy
  • Provide customer support — Respond to your feedback and support requests
  • Ensure app security — Detect and prevent unauthorized access or abuse
🛡️ Important

We never use your personal data for targeted advertising. Your pet's photos are never shared with advertisers.

5. Data Storage & Security

We take your data security seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit — All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections
  • Encryption at rest — Stored data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Access controls — Strict role-based access to user data among our team
  • Regular audits — Periodic security reviews and vulnerability assessments

5.1 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

5.2 Data Location

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located in the United States, South Korea, or other countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.
